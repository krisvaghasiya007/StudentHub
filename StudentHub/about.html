About the Portal

StudentHub is a web portal developed for students to simplify academic activities and improve communication between students and faculty. The portal provides easy access to course information, assignments, college events, student profiles, and important announcements through a single platform.

Objectives

  • Provide easy access to academic information.
  • Improve communication between students and faculty.
  • Manage courses and assignments efficiently.
  • Share college notices and upcoming events.
  • Maintain student information in one place.

Benefits

  • Simple and user-friendly interface.
  • Easy navigation across all modules.
  • Quick access to academic resources.
  • Improves organization and productivity.
  • Supports efficient communication within the college.

Key Features

Feature Description
Courses View enrolled subjects and faculty details.
Assignments Track assignment deadlines and submission status.
Events Stay updated with college events and activities.
Student Profile View personal and academic information.
Contact Contact the college for assistance and support.
